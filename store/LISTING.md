# Play Store listing copy

**App name:** Tessera
**Short description (80 max):** One tile per day for anything you do daily. Streaks, sync, friends.
**Full description:**

Tessera turns any daily habit into a mosaic of tiles, one per day. Tap once when you've done it; watch the year fill in.

• Track anything: gym, reading, water, meditation, code, or whatever you decide.
• Streaks and monthly counts for each task, plus a year-at-a-glance grid.
• Earn tiles on streak milestones; bank streak freezes for the days life gets in the way.
• Press and slide across the calendar to log a whole stretch at once.
• Sign in with Google to sync across your devices. Works offline; catches up later.
• Share a task with friends: everyone keeps their own record, and a leaderboard shows how you stack up.

No ads and no tracking. Signing in is optional; it stores your tasks, your Google account name and email, and, for shared tasks, your group membership, so everything syncs across your devices.
