## Watch items (Needs action)
- The policy says deletion removes everything on the server, but other-device KV sessions (name, email) and the `del:` marker persist up to 90 days; delete them, or say so in privacy.html "How long it is kept" (relevant at closed/production).
- Terms acceptance is a passive notice; consider an affirmative tap before create/join, fix the "By joining" wording in the Share sheet, and add a Report route for group names seen in preview.
- Delete page is email-only; consider a form.
- Wallet is client-authoritative (`worker/src/routes/sync.ts`); selling tiles or freezes would require Play Billing and a server-authoritative balance.
- `android:allowBackup="true"` (`android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml:35`): decide whether Android auto-backup is intended.
- Rename the support mailbox, `apple-mobile-web-app-title`, Google Cloud project and OAuth consent name before publishing the consent screen.
- Re-check the current minimum target API at upload (36 is set).
- Back up the upload keystore and its plain-text password file.
- Rewarded ads, a subscription, push or a watch app each reopen Ads, Subscriptions, Notifications, Data safety and IARC.
